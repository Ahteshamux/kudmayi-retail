## 1. Kudmayi in one paragraph

**Kudmayi** (کڑمائی) is a Pakistani **groomwear** brand based in **Lahore**. It sells only menswear for weddings and formal occasions: **sherwanis, prince coats (bandhgalas), waistcoats, kurtas and suits**, plus bespoke tailoring and custom kurtas. Pieces are cut, embroidered and finished by hand by karigars, in silhouettes passed down through generations of South Asian tailoring. The brand line is *"Rooted in Pakistani craft, built for the modern groom."* Customers can visit the store on M.M. Alam Road in Gulberg, or order online at kudmayi.pk and finish the order on WhatsApp. Kudmayi ships across Pakistan and abroad on request.

**Positioning:** premium, heritage-led groomwear for the Pakistani groom, covering every wedding event from Mehndi and Nikah through Barat and Walima.

## 4. Service and policies (✅ from the site)

- **Ready-to-ship:** dispatched within **3–5 working days** anywhere in Pakistan.
- **Made-to-order:** cut and finished within **3–4 weeks** of confirmation.
- **Free alterations:** any piece can be tailored to the customer's measurements **at no extra cost** before it ships.
- **International shipping:** on request. The customer gets a quote on WhatsApp.
- **Exchanges:** unaltered ready-to-ship pieces can be exchanged within **7 days** of delivery. Altered and made-to-order pieces are final sale.
- **How ordering works:** browse on kudmayi.pk, add to cart, check out. The order goes to Kudmayi on WhatsApp, where the team confirms size, delivery and payment.
- **Sizes:** standard S–XXL, plus custom measurements (chest, length, shoulder).
- **Store pick-up:** some pieces are marked as available in store.

## 5. Who the customer is

1. **The groom (25–35)**, in Lahore first, then across Punjab and Pakistan. He is planning outfits for Mehndi, Nikah, Barat and Walima, and is comparing sherwani versus prince coat.
2. **Family members buying for the groom**, often the mother, sister or the bride's side. They search in Urdu or Roman Urdu.
3. **Groom's brothers, friends and baraatis** who want coordinated waistcoats or kurtas.
4. **Overseas Pakistanis (UK, UAE, USA, Canada)** shopping for a wedding back home, or for one abroad. International shipping applies.
5. **Formal / Eid buyers** looking for premium kurtas and waistcoats outside wedding season.

**Seasonality:** Pakistani wedding season peaks **October–March**, with a big spike in **November–December**. Eid ul-Fitr and Eid ul-Adha bring kurta and waistcoat demand. Book budget for those windows.

## 8. Brand voice

- **Tone:** refined, warm and confident. Heritage without being stuffy. Speak to the groom as a guest of a tailoring house, not as a bargain shopper.
- **Use words like:** hand-embroidered, crafted, karigar, heritage, tailored, bespoke, made around you, for the occasion.
- **Avoid:** "cheap", "sale!!!", heavy discount framing, lots of exclamation marks, all-caps shouting, emojis in headlines.
- **Visual identity:** dark espresso brown, ivory and antique brass/gold. Editorial photography of grooms in heritage interiors.
